🌾 Crop Varieties

Each crop has unique growth requirements, profitability, and compatibility with different soil types.

🌾
Rice
Water-loving staple crop

Seed Cost: 8 coins | Growth Time: 20 seconds | Base Price: 25 coins

Rice requires abundant water and thrives in alluvial soil. High water needs make it vulnerable during droughts but very profitable when well-maintained.

Best Soil: Alluvial (90% yield)
Water Need: High (1.2x normal)
Fertilizer Optimal: 60-80%
🌾
Wheat
Hardy grain crop

Seed Cost: 10 coins | Growth Time: 15 seconds | Base Price: 30 coins

Versatile crop that grows well in various soil types. Moderate water requirements and good disease resistance make it a reliable choice.

Best Soil: Alluvial (85% yield)
Water Need: Normal (1.0x)
Fertilizer Optimal: 40-70%
🤍
Cotton
Valuable cash crop

Seed Cost: 15 coins | Growth Time: 30 seconds | Base Price: 50 coins

High-value crop requiring deep, nutrient-rich soil. Long growth period but excellent returns. Performs best in black soil conditions.

Best Soil: Black (95% yield)
Water Need: High (1.5x normal)
Fertilizer Optimal: 70-90%
🥜
Groundnut
Drought-resistant legume

Seed Cost: 6 coins | Growth Time: 12 seconds | Base Price: 20 coins

Fast-growing, drought-tolerant crop that fixes nitrogen in soil. Excellent for challenging conditions and quick profits.

Best Soil: Red (85% yield)
Water Need: Low (0.8x normal)
Fertilizer Optimal: 30-60%

🌦️ Weather System

Dynamic weather events create realistic farming challenges and opportunities. Adapt your strategy!

☀️
Clear Weather
Optimal conditions

Perfect farming conditions with normal growth rates and water requirements. Baseline for all farming activities.

Growth Rate: Normal (1.0x)
Water Need: Normal (1.0x)
Duration: Permanent
🌵
Drought
Water shortage

Severe water shortage causes crops to dry faster. Increase watering frequency to prevent crop death.

Growth Rate: Fast (1.2x)
Water Need: High (2.0x)
Duration: 25 seconds
🌧️
Heavy Rain
Flooding risk

Excessive rainfall can waterlog crops. Reduce watering to prevent overhydration damage.

Growth Rate: Slow (0.8x)
Water Need: Low (0.5x)
Duration: 20 seconds
🔥
Heatwave
Extreme temperatures

High temperatures accelerate growth but dramatically increase water consumption. Monitor closely!

Growth Rate: Very Fast (1.5x)
Water Need: Very High (2.5x)
Duration: 30 seconds
❄️
Cold Snap
Freezing temperatures

Sudden cold weather slows crop metabolism. Growth nearly stops, but water needs remain normal.

Growth Rate: Very Slow (0.6x)
Water Need: Normal (1.0x)
Duration: 35 seconds

⏰ Water Timeline (varies by weather)

💧
Thirsty Stage
Crops become thirsty after 12-18 seconds without water (varies by weather conditions).
🌵
Dry Stage
Reached after 24-36 seconds without water. Crops start showing stress symptoms.
🥀
Wilted Stage
After 36-54 seconds without water. Visual drooping effect and severe yield loss.
💀
Death Stage
After 48-72 seconds without water. Complete crop loss - must replant from scratch.

💡 Pro Farming Tips

Advanced strategies and techniques to maximize your farming success and profitability.

🎯
Optimal Crop Spacing
Maintain at least 40 pixels between crops to prevent competition for nutrients and ensure proper growth. Overcrowded plants suffer reduced yields.
🌦️
Weather Adaptation
During drought, focus on drought-resistant crops like groundnut. In rainy weather, reduce watering frequency to prevent overhydration.
🏞️
Soil-Crop Matching
Plant rice in alluvial soil (90% yield), cotton in black soil (95% yield), and groundnut in red soil (85% yield) for maximum profits.
💰
Economic Strategy
Start with fast-growing, low-cost crops like groundnut to build capital, then invest in high-value cotton for long-term profits.
🧪
Fertilizer Timing
Apply fertilizer immediately after planting for best results. Aim for 50-60% fertilizer level - the optimal range for maximum yield bonus.
⏰
Water Management
Check crop status regularly in the sidebar. Water before crops become "dry" to maintain optimal growth rates and prevent yield losses.
🌱
Growth Monitoring
Use the growing crops panel to track individual plant health. Mature crops glow golden - harvest immediately for best prices.
🚜
Field Preparation
Plough large areas at once using smooth, curved motions. Well-ploughed soil shows darker color and furrow lines for easy identification.
📊
Profit Maximization
Healthy crops earn 120% base price, while dead crops earn nothing. Maintaining good health is more profitable than planting more crops.
🌾
Harvest Timing
Don't delay harvesting mature crops - they continue consuming water without growing further. Harvest immediately for optimal resource efficiency.
🔄
Crop Rotation
After harvesting, you can immediately replant in the same location. Mix fast and slow-growing crops for continuous income streams.
🎲
Risk Management
Don't put all resources into one crop type. Diversify with different crops to minimize losses from weather events or poor management.
